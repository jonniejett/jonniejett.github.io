Nyack Bagels

It's the morning rush on Main Street. Slide fresh bagels down the counters before hungry customers reach you.

Each counter has its own bagel: Everything, Sesame, Poppy and Plain. The bubble shows how many a customer wants. Send them all, and they tip when they're full.

Catch the empty plates when they slide back, and don't let a bagel fly off the end of the counter.

Phone: tap a counter to move there, tap it again to slide a bagel. Or use the buttons. Keyboard: ↑ ↓ to move, Space to slide.
