Route 59 Crossing

Help Rocky the raccoon get home! Cross busy Route 59, then hop across the Hudson on logs and rafts to the Nyack docks.

👆 Tap the screen to hop forward, or swipe any direction. The arrow buttons work too.

🚗 Don't get hit. 🌊 Don't fall in the river: ride the logs and rafts, but watch out for rafts that sink!

🏠 Fill all 5 docks to clear the level. 🥯 Grab a bagel in a dock for bonus points.
