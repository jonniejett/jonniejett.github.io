Knight's Peril

The emerald dragon Cindermaw has locked old wizard Bram in a cage deep inside Blackthorn Keep. Guide Sir Rowan through 10 deadly rooms to set him free!

⚡ When danger strikes, a glowing icon flashes. Hit that move before the ring runs out:

▲ jump · ▼ duck · ◀ back off · ▶ dash ahead · ⚔️ swing your sword

🔀 Your choices matter! Pick which doors to take, whether to fight or slip past enemies, and which treasures to grab. Different choices lead to different rooms and different endings.

Swipe works too, and tapping the screen swings the sword. Wrong move or too slow? Sir Rowan meets a silly end and tries again. You have 5 lives.
