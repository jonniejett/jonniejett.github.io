Diner Rush

It's the breakfast rush at the Rockland Diner and you're working all four counters!

👆 Tap a counter to run there and slide a coffee down it. Or use ▲ ▼ to switch counters and ☕ SLIDE to send one.

☕ Each coffee pushes a customer back toward the door. Push them all the way out to serve them.

🔁 When a customer finishes, they slide the empty mug back. Be at that counter to catch it!

❌ You lose a cup if a customer reaches you, a coffee slides off the end with nobody to catch it, or you miss an empty mug. 💵 Tap tips for bonus points.
