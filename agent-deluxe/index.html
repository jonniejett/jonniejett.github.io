Skyline Agent Deluxe

A 5-stage campaign with new buildings, new enemies, traps and power-ups.

You've dropped onto the roof of an enemy tower. Work your way down, grab every 📁 secret file behind the red doors, and escape in the getaway car in the garage.

◀ ▶ walk · ▲ jump or go into a door · ▼ duck · FIRE shoot

🚪 Duck into any door to hide from bullets, then press any button to step back out. 🪜 Every floor has an UP escalator at one end and a DOWN escalator at the other. Just walk onto one to ride it.

💡 Jump and shoot a ceiling lamp to drop it on agents below. Duck under high shots, jump over low ones.

✨ Power-ups: ⚡ rapid fire · 🔱 triple shot · 🛡️ shield · 👻 cloak · ❤️ extra life. 🦶 Jump into an agent to kick him!

Elevators go up and down on their own and stop at every floor. Step in, and step off when it stops at the floor you want. The map on the right shows where the files are.
